PeopleOps HR Simulation

Managing People, Risk and Business Performance

You are the HR Manager of Northstar Consumer Group. You will receive a sequence of realistic workplace issues that require you to gather information, exercise HR judgement and make decisions under competing business and people pressures.

About Northstar Consumer Group

Northstar Consumer Group is a fictional, Singapore-headquartered consumer business with approximately 420 employees. The company operates across sales, operations, finance, marketing, technology and corporate functions, serving customers through a mix of retail, distribution and digital channels.

Northstar has grown quickly and is now moving from an entrepreneurial way of working toward a more structured and professionally managed organisation. Commercial expectations remain high, while managers are under increasing pressure to deliver results, control costs and retain capable employees.

For HR, this creates a challenging environment. Policies and management practices are not always applied consistently, managers differ in their people-management capability, and employees increasingly expect fairness, transparency and flexibility. At the same time, senior leaders expect HR to protect the organisation from risk without becoming an obstacle to business performance.

Your position: You are Northstar's HR Manager, reporting to the CHRO. Your role is not simply to process HR requests. You are expected to investigate situations, challenge assumptions where necessary, advise managers and make decisions that balance people, performance, risk, culture and cost.

Purpose of the activity

Develop practical HR decision-making by balancing employee experience, organisational performance, legal and HR risk, management expectations, culture and cost.

How the simulation works

  • Scenarios unfold one at a time.
  • Speak to stakeholders before deciding.
  • New information may emerge while you investigate.
  • Earlier decisions can create later consequences.
Your objective
Maintain the highest possible score across all components. Strong HR judgement means sustaining balanced outcomes rather than maximising one score at the expense of the others.

Your scorecard

Employee Trust · Business Performance · HR / Legal Risk · Manager Confidence · People Culture · Budget Remaining

All non-budget measures begin at 50. For HR / Legal Risk, lower is better.
